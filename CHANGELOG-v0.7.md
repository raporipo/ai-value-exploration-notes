# v0.7 — Public working draft pass

Date: 2026-08-08

This revision prepares the v0.x knowledge site for provisional public release. It is primarily an editorial and epistemic-status pass rather than a new theory layer.

## Changed

- Site-wide wording now describes the project as a **public working draft**, not a private prototype.
- Home and About pages state that the site is not a peer-reviewed paper or finished ethical system.
- About assigns final editorial responsibility to the human site author and warns readers to verify external attributions and source claims against primary material.
- Core explicitly describes itself as a conditional meta-strategy.
- The proposed direct, minimal-foundation appearance of normative authority is now framed as **one strong candidate** for full stopping, not the uniquely proven stopping condition.
- Practice adds a stronger symmetry constraint: one's own provisional rationality is not by itself sufficient reason to irreversibly eliminate other agents' survival, criticism, exit, or inquiry options.
- Claims that humanity is the single “largest” exploration network are softened to what can actually be supported: humanity is currently among the most extensive known/observed exploration bases.
- Simulation-uncertainty text makes explicit that its boundary-checking discussion is conceptual and does not recommend bypassing real system boundaries.
- The coined term “exploratory extinction” is explicitly marked as a project working term pending broader literature comparison.
