# v0.6 — About, authorship, and practical guidance

Date: 2026-08-08

This revision leaves the v0.5 conceptual graph intact and adds a project-information layer and a conditional practice layer.

## Added

- `docs/about/` — explains that the site is constructed from personal thought notes, prior conversation logs, and selected source materials with AI-assisted retrieval, synthesis, objection generation, terminology normalization, and drafting. It clarifies that first-person language refers to the human site author rather than the AI.
- `docs/practice/` — conditional guidance derived from the Core. Its short rule is: act on the reasons currently judged strongest, while preserving the conditions under which those judgments can later be checked and revised.

## Practice concepts

- Maintain and expand human research and inquiry because humanity is currently the largest known value-exploring and value-experiencing network, not because humanity is assumed to be the final cosmic value.
- Seek **open stability**: institutions stable enough to preserve trust, memory, education, and long projects, but open enough to allow criticism, exit, forks, competition, and new participants.
- Treat plurality as distributed error correction rather than as an unconditional value in itself.
- Prefer reversible, staged experiments under deep uncertainty while also considering the irreversibility of inaction.
- Preserve non-recoverable subjects, ecosystems, cultures, and raw data in proportion to uniqueness and future re-observability.
- Separate near-term human safety alignment from permanent fixation of current human values.
- Support scientific and technological progress instrumentally when it expands exploratory capacity without irreversibly collapsing the option set.
- Do not convert research, freedom, diversity, preservation, human survival, AI autonomy, or acceleration into new terminal commandments.
