# v0.8 — English translation layer

Date: 2026-08-08

This revision adds the first complete English working translation of the public argument graph.

- Adds `docs/en/` counterparts for the home page, Core, all Theses, all Explorations, Practice, Questions, Glossary, and About.
- Keeps Japanese as the source language and labels English explicitly as a working translation.
- Adds bidirectional language navigation and `hreflang` metadata (`ja`, `en`, `x-default`).
- Standardizes several English project terms, including **explorability**, **best explanation reached through inference**, **foundational normativity**, **open stability**, **goal skepticism**, and **exploratory extinction**.
- Updates `README.md`, `llms.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, and the 404 page for bilingual publication.

No major conceptual change to the Japanese v0.7 argument is intended in this revision. Where English lacks a direct equivalent, wording is chosen to preserve the argumentative role rather than literal surface form.
